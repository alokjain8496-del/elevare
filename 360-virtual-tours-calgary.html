What property types are suitable for 360 tours?

Detached homes, townhomes, condos, new builds, and many rental or investment properties can benefit from a virtual walkthrough.

Can a 360 tour be combined with floor plans?

Yes. A tour and floor plan often work well together because buyers can both walk through and understand the layout.

How should a property be prepared?

Rooms should be clean, well lit, and ready to photograph before the appointment begins.
