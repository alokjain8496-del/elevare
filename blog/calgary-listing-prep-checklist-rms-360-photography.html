Calgary Listing Prep Checklist: How to Prepare a Home for RMS Measurement, 360° Tour & Photography

A practical, shareable checklist Calgary Realtors can forward to sellers 48 hours before shoot day so HDR photography, 360° panoramas, and RECA RMS exterior measurements go smoothly.

Top 4 Prep Wins Before Your Technician Arrives

  • Lights On, Blinds Open: Turn on every overhead light, under-cabinet strip, and lamp, and pull blinds fully open for bright, balanced HDR frames.
  • Clear Every Surface: Remove countertop clutter, dish soap, fridge magnets, toilet items, garbage bins, and visible charging cords.
  • Unlock Full Access for RMS: Unlock mechanical rooms, walk-in closets, basement suites, garage doors, and exterior side gates so exterior perimeter verification is fast and accurate.
  • Empty the Driveway: Park vehicles down the street and roll garbage/compost bins out of sight for crisp curb-appeal exterior shots.

Great Calgary real estate photography and 360° virtual tours start before the camera tripod ever comes out of the bag. Because a 360° panorama captures the entire room in every direction—and an Alberta RECA RMS measurement requires clean access to interior rooms and exterior foundation walls—a 30-minute prep routine makes a dramatic difference in final quality.

Realtors are welcome to share this checklist directly with homeowners 24 to 48 hours ahead of their Elevares Studio appointment.

1. Exterior Curb Appeal & RMS Foundation Access

  • Move all vehicles out of the driveway and away from the front curb.
  • Tuck away black/blue/green City of Calgary bins, hoses, lawn tools, and snow shovels.
  • Unlock side-yard gates and clear snow or stored items away from foundation corners so our technician can verify exterior wall dimensions cleanly.
  • Remove BBQ covers and arrange patio furniture neatly during spring, summer, and fall shoots.

2. Kitchen, Dining & Living Areas

  • Keep the kitchen island completely clear—remove paper towels, dish racks, small appliances, sponges, and oven-handle towels.
  • Clear refrigerator doors of magnets, calendars, and school artwork.
  • Straighten dining chairs, plump sofa cushions, and remove oversized corner floor clutter that blocks wide-angle sightlines.
  • Switch off ceiling fans (spinning blades blur in HDR exposures) and turn off TV screens.
  • Replace any burnt-out bulbs and match warm vs. daylight bulb colours in multi-bulb fixtures where possible.

3. Bedrooms, Bathrooms & Walk-In Closets

  • Make all beds neatly with simple linens and hide phone chargers, tissue boxes, and medications inside nightstands.
  • Clear bathroom vanities completely—tuck toothbrushes, shampoo bottles, bath mats, and wastebaskets out of sight.
  • Close all toilet lids and hang fresh, matching towels neatly.
  • Leave bedroom and walk-in closet doors open (or unlocked) so laser measurements capture full room geometry accurately.

4. Basements, Walkouts & Utility Rooms

Even when a basement is unfinished or partially developed, our floor plan drafting and RECA RMS verification require clean access to lower levels, furnace rooms, and secondary suite entries. Ensure stairways are clear, lights work, and tenant-occupied suites have advance notice.

5. Preparing Vacant Homes & New Builds

For empty resale homes, spec builds, or new infills, ensure construction dust is swept, builder stickers are removed from windows, and utilities (power and lighting) are live. Want empty rooms to feel warm and buyer-ready online? Pair your shoot with our 24-hour Calgary virtual staging service.

Calgary Shoot-Day Prep FAQs

How long should sellers plan for a full photo + RMS + 360° tour appointment?
Most Calgary single-family homes and townhomes take 60 to 90 minutes on site for combined HDR photography, RECA RMS measurements, floor plans, and a calibrated iGUIDE 360° tour.
Should homeowners or pets stay home during the shoot?
For fastest capture—especially during 360° panoramas where the entire room is visible—it is best for homeowners to step out or relax in one out-of-frame area while pets are secured or taken for a walk.
What if it snows or rains on photography day in Calgary?
Interior photography, 360° tours, and RMS measurements proceed normally in any weather, and we apply natural blue-sky replacements or schedule quick exterior touch-ups if heavy snow obscures curb appeal.
When will our finished photos, RMS report, and tour arrive?
Elevares Studio delivers your edited web and print HDR photos, RECA-compliant RMS report, floor plans, and iGUIDE 360° link within a guaranteed 24-hour turnaround.
