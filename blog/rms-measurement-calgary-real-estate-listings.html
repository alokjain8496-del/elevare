Alberta RECA RMS Rules Explained: Why Accurate Measurements Matter in Calgary

A practical Calgary guide to exterior wall vs. condo paint-to-paint rules, walkout basement exclusions, ceiling-height minimums, and RECA’s 2% variance tolerance—plus how floor plans and iGUIDE 360° tours turn compliance into buyer confidence.

Key Alberta RECA RMS Rules at a Glance

  • Detached & Townhomes vs. Condos: Detached homes, duplexes, and townhomes are measured around the exterior foundation wall; apartment condos with shared demising walls are measured strictly interior paint-to-paint.
  • Below-Grade & Walkouts Excluded: Any level partially below grade—including fully finished walkout basements and bi-level lower floors—never counts in above-grade RMS totals (report it separately).
  • Ceiling Height Minimums: Qualifying levels require a minimum 2.13 m (7 ft) ceiling height, with sloped/vaulted areas included down to 1.52 m (5 ft) if the room reaches 2.13 m elsewhere.
  • 2% Variance Tolerance: Advertised RMS measurements must stay within 2% of actual property size, making calibrated laser and iGUIDE lidar capture essential before MLS® launch.

What Is Alberta’s Residential Measurement Standard (RMS)?

RMS stands for Residential Measurement Standard, the uniform framework administered by the Real Estate Council of Alberta (RECA). While RECA does not force every listing to advertise square footage, whenever an Alberta real estate licensee measures and advertises residential property size, the RMS principles must be followed.

Calgary’s housing stock makes a single standard critical. On any given street you can find 1960s bi-levels, inner-city infills with vaulted bonus rooms, hillside walkouts, stacked townhomes, and concrete condo towers. Without a shared rulebook, two nearly identical homes could advertise dramatically different square footage simply because one counted an attached garage or walkout basement.

Core Alberta RECA RMS Rules Every Calgary Listing Must Follow

Whether you are preparing a custom infill in Altadore, a walkout in Cranston, or a condo downtown, these five RECA RMS rules determine exactly what counts toward advertised above-grade area:

Property Feature Alberta RECA RMS Rule How It Appears on MLS®
Detached, Duplex & Townhomes Measured around the exterior perimeter at foundation level, including exterior wall thickness. Included in Above-Grade RMS Total
Apartment Condos Measured strictly from interior paint-to-paint wall surfaces (excluding shared demising walls and common corridors). Included in Above-Grade RMS Total
Basements, Walkouts & Bi-Levels Any level even partially below grade—even with full-size windows or walkout doors—is classified as below grade. Excluded from RMS; listed separately as below-grade area
Sloped / Vaulted Ceilings Floor levels must reach 2.13 m (7 ft). Sloped zones qualify down to 1.52 m (5 ft) if the room reaches 2.13 m elsewhere. Qualifying footprint included; low-clearance areas excluded
Garages, Decks & Open-to-Below Attached garages, balconies, patios, unheated sunrooms, and open-to-below stair or foyer voids are non-livable area. Excluded from Above-Grade RMS Total

Frequently Asked Questions About Calgary RMS Measurements

What is RMS measurement in Calgary real estate?
RMS stands for Residential Measurement Standard, the mandatory Alberta standard established by the Real Estate Council of Alberta (RECA) whenever a real estate licensee measures and advertises a residential property’s size.
Are walkout basements or bi-level lower floors included in above-grade RMS square footage?
No. Under Alberta RECA RMS rules, any level that is even partially below grade—including fully finished walkout basements, bi-level lower floors, and split-level lower levels—must be excluded from above-grade RMS square footage and reported separately as below-grade developed area.
How are Calgary apartment condos measured differently from detached homes under RMS?
Detached homes, duplexes, and townhomes are measured around the exterior perimeter at foundation level (including exterior wall thickness). Apartment-style condominiums and stacked units with shared demising walls are measured strictly from interior paint-to-paint wall surfaces.
What is the minimum ceiling height required to count toward Alberta RMS area?
A floor level must have a minimum floor-to-ceiling height of 2.13 metres (7 feet) to qualify. In rooms with sloped or vaulted ceilings, areas with at least 1.52 metres (5 feet) of height may be included only if the room reaches 2.13 metres (7 feet) elsewhere.
