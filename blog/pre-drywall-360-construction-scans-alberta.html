Pre-Drywall 360° Scan Essentials for Alberta Builders

  • Ideal Capture Window: Book right after framing, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC rough-in inspections pass—immediately before insulation and drywall boarding start.
  • Zero Exploratory Drywall Cuts: Warranty coordinators locate concealed pipes, shutoff valves, backing, and junction boxes remotely in seconds.
  • Homeowner “X-Ray” Handover: Sharing the browser-based iGUIDE link at possession differentiates your brand and helps owners mount TVs or develop basements safely.
  • 24-Hour Turnaround: Elevares Studio delivers single-family and multi-family scans across Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, and Olds within 24 hours.

Once insulation and drywall go up on a custom home or multi-family build, every stud, backing board, plumbing stack, and electrical chase disappears behind paint. Six months after possession, a single mystery leak, missed pot-light wire, or heavy cabinetry mount can trigger costly exploratory drywall cuts.

That is why leading Alberta custom builders and developers—including partners like Ambedo Homes, Light House Homes, and RPMI—now include a pre-drywall 360° construction scan as a standard quality-control milestone on every build.

What a Pre-Drywall 360° Digital Twin Actually Captures

Unlike scattered smartphone photos stored in someone’s camera roll, a calibrated iGUIDE pre-drywall scan stitches high-resolution 360° panoramas directly onto a laser-measured 2D floor plan. Anyone on your team can click any room in a web browser, pan 360 degrees from floor joists to ceiling trusses, and inspect exact behind-the-wall rough-ins:

  • Structural framing, load-bearing headers, point loads, and blocking/backing locations for cabinetry or wall-mounted TVs.
  • Electrical home runs, low-voltage conduit, recessed lighting boxes, and junction locations.
  • PEX water supply lines, ABS drain stacks, cleanouts, shutoff valves, and in-floor radiant tubing.
  • HVAC supply/return trunk lines, bath exhaust vents, and fire-stopping assemblies.

Warranty Claim Protection & Trade Accountability

Even one prevented exploratory drywall tear-out, mud/tape repair, and paint repaint easily pays for a pre-drywall scan several times over. Site supervisors and warranty managers use the digital twin to:

  • Verify trade rough-in compliance remotely before approving milestone draws.
  • Confirm whether backing, rough-in plumbing, or wiring was installed as specified if a dispute arises months later.
  • Pinpoint exact nail-plate, stud, and pipe locations before dispatching service technicians.

The Homeowner “X-Ray” Possession Handover

Custom homebuyers love seeing the craftsmanship behind their walls. Including the permanent iGUIDE link inside your buyer possession binder turns technical documentation into a high-value brand differentiator—giving homeowners confidence when mounting heavy mirrors, installing garage storage, or planning future basement developments.

When the home reaches completion, pairing that archive with finished HDR real estate photography and RECA-compliant RMS measurements completes your marketing package in one relationship.

Builder Pre-Drywall 360° Scan FAQs

How long does a pre-drywall 360° scan take on site?
A typical single-family custom home takes 45 to 75 minutes with minimal trade disruption. Multi-family buildings are captured floor-by-floor around active crew schedules.
How long does the iGUIDE pre-drywall link remain accessible?
The hosted digital twin remains accessible online for warranty teams and homeowners, and downloadable offline floor plans and measurement summaries are delivered within 24 hours.
