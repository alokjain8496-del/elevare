Schedule your pre-drywall iGUIDE scan immediately after framing, electrical runs, plumbing rough-ins, and HVAC ductwork pass inspection—just before insulation and drywall boarding begin. A pre-drywall 360° scan creates a permanent, millimeter-accurate archive of every stud bay, plumbing stack, shutoff, junction box, and electrical chase behind finished drywall. When a warranty ticket comes in months later, project managers can inspect behind-the-wall layouts remotely and resolve issues without cutting exploratory holes.

Every measurement strictly follows Alberta RECA RMS principles: measuring exterior foundation walls for detached and townhome properties (paint-to-paint for apartment condos), excluding below-grade and walkout levels from above-grade RMS totals, applying the 2.13 m (7 ft) minimum ceiling height rule, and staying well within RECA’s 2% variance tolerance.
