Every residential measurement strictly follows the Real Estate Council of Alberta (RECA) Residential Measurement Standard—measuring detached homes and townhomes around the exterior foundation wall, apartment-style condos interior paint-to-paint, reporting below-grade walkout and basement levels separately from above-grade RMS totals, and adhering to the 2.13 m (7 ft) ceiling height and 2% accuracy tolerance rules.
