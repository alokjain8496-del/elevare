Common Questions From Builders & Agents

Everything you need to know about scheduling pre-drywall construction scans, RMS measurements, and 24-hour delivery across Calgary and Alberta.

Book a Site Scan
When should builders schedule a pre-drywall 360° construction scan?
The ideal window is right after framing, electrical runs, plumbing rough-ins, and HVAC ductwork pass inspection—immediately before insulation and drywall boarding begin. That way every stud, pipe, and junction box is captured cleanly in one site visit.
How fast is the turnaround for pre-drywall scans and certified RMS floor plans?
Elevares Studio delivers pre-drywall 360° construction scans, iGUIDE virtual tours, certified RMS measurements, and dimensioned floor plans within a standard 24-hour turnaround so your site schedule and MLS® launch stay on track.
How does a pre-drywall scan protect builders during warranty claims?
Your team keeps a permanent, navigable digital twin showing exact rough-in routes behind finished drywall. If a warranty ticket arises, supervisors can check pipe paths, shutoffs, blocking, and electrical lines remotely before dispatching trades or cutting walls.
Can homeowners use the scan as an “X-Ray” blueprint after possession?
Yes. Builders frequently include the interactive iGUIDE link in their possession package so homeowners can verify stud spacing, backing, plumbing stacks, and electrical runs before mounting cabinetry, artwork, or planning future renovations.
Do you scan multi-family developments outside Calgary, such as Olds, AB?
Yes. Based in southeast Calgary, Elevares Studio regularly travels across Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, Chestermere, and Central Alberta communities like Olds—including multi-family developments such as our 4510 51st Ave project.
