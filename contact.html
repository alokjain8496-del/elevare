What to Include When Booking a Site Scan

Whether you are coordinating framing and rough-in milestones for a multi-family project or preparing a Calgary MLS® listing, sharing a few quick details helps us confirm your appointment right away.

How far in advance should builders book a pre-drywall 360° scan?
We recommend reaching out 2 to 4 days before insulation and drywall boarding begin, right as electrical runs, plumbing rough-ins, and framing inspections wrap up. Same-week and next-day slots are often available across Calgary and Olds.
What deliverables are included in the 24-hour turnaround?
Standard 24-hour delivery covers your interactive iGUIDE 360° tour link, dimensioned 2D floor plans, certified Alberta RMS measurement summary (for finished listings), and downloadable assets ready for MLS®, builder portals, or homeowner handover packages.
Do you offer volume pricing for custom builders and multi-family developers?
Yes. We partner with Calgary and Alberta builders—including Ambedo Homes, Light House Homes, and RPMI—on recurring pre-drywall scans, multi-unit developments, and finished showhome media packages. Mention your project volume when requesting a quote.
How long does an on-site scan take?
A typical single-family residence or pre-drywall rough-in capture takes 45 to 75 minutes on site with minimal disruption to trades. Multi-family buildings are scheduled floor by floor around active trade windows.

Elevares Studio specializes in pre-drywall 360° construction scans, RECA-compliant RMS measurements, dimensioned floor plans, and real estate photography across Calgary and Alberta. Every project is backed by calibrated iGUIDE capture and a reliable 24-hour standard turnaround.
