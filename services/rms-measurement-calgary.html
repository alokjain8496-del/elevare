Alberta RECA Residential Measurement Standard

Four Alberta RMS Rules Every Calgary Listing Must Follow

Accurate MLS® square footage starts with applying the right boundary, grade level, ceiling height, and accuracy tolerance for each property type.

Read Full RECA RMS Guide
Rule 01 · Property Boundaries

Exterior Foundation Walls vs. Paint-to-Paint

For detached homes, duplexes, and townhomes, RMS area is calculated around the exterior perimeter at foundation level (including exterior wall thickness). For apartment-style condominiums and stacked units with shared demising walls, measurements are taken strictly from interior paint-to-paint wall surfaces.

Rule 02 · Grade Separation

Exclusion of Below-Grade & Walkout Levels

Only levels completely above grade qualify for the advertised above-grade RMS total. Basements, split-level lower floors, bi-level lower levels, and walkout basements—even when fully finished with walkout doors and full-size windows—are excluded from above-grade RMS area and itemized separately as below-grade developed space.

Rule 03 · Vertical Clearance

2.13 m (7 ft) Minimum Ceiling Height Rule

To count toward RMS square footage, a floor level must have a minimum floor-to-ceiling height of 2.13 metres (7 feet). In rooms with vaulted or sloped ceilings—such as bonus rooms or character attics—areas with a height of at least 1.52 metres (5 feet) are included only where the room also reaches 2.13 metres (7 feet) elsewhere.

Rule 04 · Accuracy Standard

2% Variance Tolerance & Lidar Verification

RECA requires residential measurements to remain within a strict 2% variance tolerance of actual property size. Elevares Studio combines calibrated iGUIDE infrared lidar sweeps, exterior wall thickness verification, and post-scan drafting audits to keep every Calgary measurement well inside RECA compliance limits.

What Is Excluded From Above-Grade RMS Totals?

In addition to below-grade and walkout levels, RECA RMS excludes attached or detached garages, open-to-below foyer and stairwell voids, unheated porches or sunrooms, balconies, decks, and non-livable mechanical closets. What is RMS measurement in Calgary real estate?
RMS stands for Residential Measurement Standard—the mandatory Alberta measurement framework administered by the Real Estate Council of Alberta (RECA) whenever a real estate licensee measures and advertises residential property size.
How are detached homes measured differently from apartment condos under Alberta RECA RMS?
Detached homes, duplexes, and townhomes are measured around the exterior perimeter at foundation level (including exterior wall thickness). Apartment-style condominiums and stacked units with shared demising walls are measured strictly from interior paint-to-paint wall surfaces.
Are walkout basements or bi-level lower floors included in above-grade RMS totals?
No. Under RECA RMS rules, any level that is even partially below grade—including fully finished walkout basements, bi-level lower levels, and split-level lower floors—must be excluded from above-grade RMS square footage and itemized separately as below-grade developed area.
