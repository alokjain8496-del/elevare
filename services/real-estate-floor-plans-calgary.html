What file formats are included with Elevares Studio floor plans?
You receive high-resolution print-ready PDFs, crisp web-ready PNG and JPG images for MLS® and social media, and optional scalable vector/SVG exports—delivered cleanly within 24 hours.
Do your floor plans separate above-grade RMS space from below-grade basements?
Yes. Every multi-level Calgary floor plan clearly distinguishes above-grade RECA RMS square footage from below-grade developed or unfinished basement levels, plus excluded garages and decks.
Can floor plans be branded with my Realtor or homebuilder logo?
Yes. We deliver both unbranded MLS®-compliant versions for CREB/Realtor.ca and branded versions featuring your brokerage or builder contact details for print brochures and handover packages.
Can I order both 2D and 3D floor plans together?
Yes. We produce clean, high-contrast 2D architectural floor plans with room-by-room dimensions as well as styled 3D layout visualizations to help buyers grasp furniture flow immediately.
