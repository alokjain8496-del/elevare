How quickly are real estate photos delivered in Calgary?
Elevares Studio delivers fully edited, colour-calibrated HDR interior and exterior photography in both MLS®-optimized web resolution and high-resolution print formats within a guaranteed 24-hour turnaround.
Do you offer twilight or dusk exterior photography?
Yes. We provide twilight and dusk exterior photography (as well as natural-looking virtual twilight conversions) to showcase architectural lighting, curb appeal, and luxury custom builds across Calgary.
Can I bundle real estate photography with RMS measurements, floor plans, and a 360° tour?
Yes. Most Calgary Realtors and custom homebuilders bundle HDR listing photography with certified RECA RMS measurements, dimensioned floor plans, and an iGUIDE 360° virtual tour in one appointment—saving both vendor coordination time and per-listing cost.
How should sellers prepare a home before photography day?
Turn on all interior lights (matching bulb colour temperatures where possible), open all blinds and curtains, clear kitchen islands and bathroom counters, remove vehicles from the driveway, and hide cords or pet items for crisp wide-angle composition.
