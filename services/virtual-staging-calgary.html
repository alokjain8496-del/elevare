How realistic does virtual staging look on Calgary MLS® listings?
Elevares Studio uses physically accurate lighting, shadows, reflections, and scale-matched furnishings so every virtually staged room looks natural alongside your real listing photography.
What MLS® disclosure best practices apply to virtually staged photos?
We follow Alberta brokerage and CREB MLS® best practices: permanent structural elements (windows, walls, views, fixtures) are never misrepresented, and we supply both the original empty photo and the staged version with clear disclosure labelling so buyers are always informed.
Which interior design styles are available?
Choose from Modern, Contemporary, Transitional, Warm Minimalist, Scandinavian, or Custom Luxury styles tailored to match Calgary infills, suburban family homes, condos, or new-build showhomes.
How fast is virtual staging delivered?
Virtually staged images are delivered within a standard 24-hour turnaround—and can be added seamlessly to any Elevares Studio photography or 360° tour booking.
