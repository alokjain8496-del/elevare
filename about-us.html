About us

Calgary & Alberta Real Estate Media

Elevares Studio.

Based in southeast Calgary, serving custom builders, multi-family developers, and Realtors with 24-hour accuracy.

Elevares Studio is a Calgary-based spatial documentation and real estate media studio built to serve custom homebuilders, multi-family developers, and top-producing Realtors across Alberta. We combine iGUIDE 360° lidar scanning, certified Alberta RMS measurement standards, detailed floor plans, virtual staging, and listing photography to make every phase of a property transparent and market-ready.

For custom and multi-family builders, our flagship service is pre-drywall 360° construction scanning—creating a permanent, measurable record of exposed framing, electrical runs, plumbing rough-ins, and HVAC pathways before insulation and drywall go up. That single site visit gives site superintendents immediate warranty protection and delivers a memorable homeowner “X-Ray” blueprint at possession.

For Calgary Realtors and property owners, we provide fast, reliable 24-hour delivery on certified RMS measurements, 360° virtual tours, dimensioned floor plans, and virtual staging—helping listings stand out online and earn stronger buyer confidence before the first showing.
