FAQs

What is RMS measurement in Calgary?

RMS stands for Residential Measurement Standard, the Alberta method used when residential property size is measured and advertised by a real estate licensee.

Is RMS required for every listing?

RECA does not require every residential listing to advertise property size. However, when an Alberta real estate licensee measures and advertises the property’s size, the Residential Measurement Standard must be followed.

Does RMS measurement include a floor plan?

Elevares includes a professional digital floor plan with RMS measurement service.
