Residential Measurement Standard (RMS) Compliance in Calgary

When an Alberta real estate licensee measures and advertises the size of a residential property, the Real Estate Council of Alberta (RECA) Residential Measurement Standard must be followed strictly.

Core RECA RMS Principles

Four Alberta RMS Rules Every Calgary Listing Must Follow

Accurate MLS® square footage starts with applying the right boundary, grade level, ceiling height, and accuracy tolerance for each property type.

Rule 01 · Property Boundaries

Exterior Foundation Walls vs. Paint-to-Paint

For detached homes, duplexes, and townhomes, RMS area is calculated around the exterior perimeter at foundation level (including exterior wall thickness). For apartment-style condominiums and stacked units with shared demising walls, measurements are taken strictly from interior paint-to-paint wall surfaces.

Rule 02 · Grade Separation

Exclusion of Below-Grade & Walkout Levels

Only levels completely above grade qualify for the advertised above-grade RMS total. Basements, split-level lower floors, bi-level lower levels, and walkout basements—even when fully finished with walkout doors and full-size windows—are excluded from above-grade RMS area and itemized separately as below-grade developed space.

Rule 03 · Vertical Clearance

2.13 m (7 ft) Minimum Ceiling Height Rule

To count toward RMS square footage, a floor level must have a minimum floor-to-ceiling height of 2.13 metres (7 feet). In rooms with vaulted or sloped ceilings—such as bonus rooms or character attics—areas with a height of at least 1.52 metres (5 feet) are included only where the room also reaches 2.13 metres (7 feet) elsewhere.

Rule 04 · Accuracy Standard

2% Variance Tolerance & Lidar Verification

RECA requires residential measurements to remain within a strict 2% variance tolerance of actual property size. Elevares Studio combines iGUIDE infrared lidar sweeps, exterior wall thickness verification, and post-scan drafting audits to keep every Calgary measurement well inside RECA compliance limits.

What Is Excluded From Above-Grade RMS Totals?

Open-to-below areas (such as two-storey foyers and staircases at the upper opening), detached or attached garages, unheated sunrooms, porches, decks, and below-grade levels are excluded from the headline RMS figure and clearly separated on your Elevares floor plan so Realtors and buyers have complete clarity.

FAQs

What is RMS measurement in Calgary?

RMS stands for Residential Measurement Standard, the Alberta method used when residential property size is measured and advertised by a real estate licensee.

Is RMS required for every listing?

RECA does not require every residential listing to advertise property size. However, when an Alberta real estate licensee measures and advertises the property’s size, the Residential Measurement Standard must be followed.
