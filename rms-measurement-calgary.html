Frequently Asked Questions — RMS Measurements Calgary

What is an RMS measurement in Calgary?

RMS (Residential Measurement Standard) is the official method required by RECA for measuring and reporting the size of residential properties in Calgary.

Is an RMS measurement required to list a property in Calgary?

Yes. Properties listed on MLS in Alberta must include RECA-compliant RMS measurements.

How long does an RMS measurement take?

Most properties take 45–90 minutes on-site, depending on size and complexity. You receive the final floor plan and report within 24 hours.

Does an RMS measurement include a floor plan?

Yes — every Elevares RMS measurement includes a professional digital floor plan at no extra charge.

How is RMS different from total square footage?

RMS only counts above-grade finished living space. Basements, garages, and unfinished areas are measured separately.
